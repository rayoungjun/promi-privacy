Delete your Promi account

Promi lets you request deletion in the app or on the web.

In the app

Sign in, open Profile → More → Request account deletion, and confirm the request.

On the web

Send an email from the address connected to your Promi account to topssata4@gmail.com with the subject Promi account deletion request. Do not send a password. We may ask you to verify that you own the account.

What happens

After verifying your request, we remove your authentication account, profile, optional profile photo, promises, reading records and community activity from active systems. Posts and replies you created will no longer appear in the community. We may retain limited records where required for safety, legal obligations or dispute handling.
