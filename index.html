Promi Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Promi is a reading promise and community app operated by the Promi developer. Contact: topssata4@gmail.com.

Information we handle

Google sign-in provides an account identifier and email address. Promi stores your display name, bio and optional profile photo; your book title, promise dates, work-hour and reminder preferences; reading session dates, outcomes, verified time and absence events; and community posts, replies, likes, follows, reports and blocks.

How we use it

We use this information to sign you in, run your reading promise, show your calendar and achievements, send requested reminders, operate the community and respond to safety reports. Your email is used for authentication and is not shown in community posts. Your profile and community activity are visible to other signed-in users. Promise details and session records are visible to your account.

Camera

During a reading check-in, Promi checks for your presence using the camera on your device. Camera images and video are processed on the device and are not saved or uploaded. Only the session result, verified time and absence count are stored.

Services and storage

Promi uses Google for sign-in and Supabase for authentication, database and optional profile photo storage. Android notification services deliver reminders on your device. We do not sell your personal data or use advertising in the current app.

Retention and control

We keep account and app data while your account is active. You can delete your own posts and replies in the app, edit your profile, and request account deletion from Profile → More → Request account deletion. See the account deletion page for a web request option and what will be removed. We may retain limited records where required for safety, legal obligations or dispute handling.

Contact

For questions about privacy or your data, email topssata4@gmail.com.
